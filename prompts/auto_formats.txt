Predicate (bounded JSON expression over trusted probe values): an integer, a boolean, {"ref": "<ProbeLabel>"} or {"op": OP, "args": [...]} with OP in eq ne lt le gt ge add sub mul (two args) and and or (two boolean args) and not (one boolean arg). Depth <= 8, at most 128 nodes. A requirement HOLDS when its predicate evaluates to true; a frame where it is false is a violation.

Requirement: {"id": "CamelCaseId", "scope": "spec" | "intent", "property_id": "<Lean property id for spec scope, else empty string>", "requirement": "<English normative statement>", "intent_quote": "<exact substring of the creator intent>", "step_scope": "each" | "final" | "after:<function signature>", "predicate": <predicate>, "origin": {"version": <int>, "source": "open" | "judge" | "repair" | "migrated", "rationale": "<why this requirement follows from intent>"}}. step_scope "each" checks the frame after deployment/setup and after every submitted action.

Probe: {"kind": "view", "label": "Label", "target": "<challenge deployment name>", "function": "name(type,...)", "arguments": ["..."]} reading one scalar uint/int/bool/address view; {"kind": "balance", "label": "Label", "target": "<challenge deployment name>"} reading a contract's ether balance; {"kind": "account_balance", "label": "Label", "account": <0-9>} reading a local account's ether balance; {"kind": "block_timestamp", "label": "Label"} reading the latest block timestamp. Gas is free on the local chain, so account balances change only by value transfers.

Argument strings: decimal integers, "true"/"false", "$account:<0-9>", "$deployment:<name>", "$contract" (first challenge deployment) or "$now+<seconds>" (latest block timestamp plus seconds).
