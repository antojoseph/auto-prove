              Limits

              What this is — and is not

              Is

              • An internal pilot of a transaction-grounded specification review loop, fully reproducible from the repository.
              • A staging page for the Yukon challenge: same objective, rules, scoring and evidence the hosted challenge will use.
              • Kernel-checked formal evidence (Lean, two independent kernels) plus concrete Anvil replays, separately labeled.

              Is not

              • A submission endpoint: no backend, no registry writes, no authenticated attribution.
              • Hosted checking: Anvil replay and Lean verification run only in the CLI/CI.
              • A security certificate, an English-fidelity proof, a model/EVM equivalence proof, or a payout mechanism.
              • Production creator approval: recorded decisions are internal-fixture reviews by the repo maintainer.
