Round 1

Formal proof: proved

model_gap — The source uses `owner.call`, which can execute an owner contract's receive hook. A one-shot hook can reenter `withdraw(10)` during an outer `withdraw(0)`. The outer transaction then pays 10 wei and leaves a 20-wei vault with 10 wei, while the model records no payout and no balance change for `withdraw(0)`. Both calls are made by the owner contract, so this does not demonstrate a violation of owner-only authority. It does show why the model cannot resolve the requested reentrancy assumption or any limit on the aggregate payout of one top-level call. The Lean observation establishes only the model result; the source-level sequence has not been replayed.
